Project Better Tomorrow

SmartSpend – Smart Daily Spending Tracker was engineered to solve the widespread financial distress experienced by students and young adults living independently. By making micro-expenditure tracking effortless, highlighting subtle weekend spending patterns, and providing safe daily spending guardrails, SmartSpend empowers youth with healthy financial literacy and mindful budgeting habits.
